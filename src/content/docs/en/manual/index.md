New to Common Voice? Follow these five steps. Each one takes a few clicks.

You can start without an account. As a guest, in a Scripted Speech dataset released under CC0, you can write one sentence at a time and record up to 100 sentences. Listen, Review, adding many sentences at once, and the other sections need an account.

## Step 1: Choose the site language {#site-language}

- Open the language button at the top right.
- ① Type your language, or ② pick it from the list.
- This changes the website text only.
- More: [the header](common-parts.md#site-language).

![The language list, with a search box ① and the languages ②](../../images/en/manual/common-parts/site-language-desktop-light.png)

## Step 2: Open Contribute {#contribute}

- ① Use the **Contribute** menu at the top.
- ② Or press **Contribute** on a card below.

![The Contribute page, with the menu ① and the section cards ②](../../images/en/manual/start-here/contribute-desktop-light.png)

On a phone, ① opens the menu and ② is a section card.

![The Contribute page on a phone, with the menu button ① and a section card ②](../../images/en/manual/start-here/contribute-phone-light.png)

## Step 3: Pick a section {#section}

- **Scripted Speech**: read short sentences out loud.
- **Spontaneous Speech**: answer questions in your own words.
- **Code Switching**: speak across two languages in one turn.
- **Asynchronous Conversations**: reply to others in moderated, topic-bound rooms.

Scripted Speech and Spontaneous Speech are open now. Code Switching and Asynchronous Conversations are opening soon.

## Step 4: Pick a dataset {#dataset}

- ② **Select a dataset** to switch the steps on. A dataset is one language.
- ① The steps are the ways you can help.
- ③ Your language is not there? Use **Request a dataset** on the Scripted Speech page, for a general or a domain-specific dataset. Requests are open for Scripted Speech only.

![A section page, with the steps ①, the dataset picker ② and the request buttons ③](../../images/en/manual/start-here/section-overview-desktop-light.png)

On a phone, ① are the steps and ② is the dataset picker.

![A section page on a phone, with the steps ① and the dataset picker ②](../../images/en/manual/start-here/section-overview-phone-light.png)

## Step 5: Do a step {#step}

- [**Write**](scripted-speech/write.md): add sentences.
- [**Review**](scripted-speech/review.md): review sentences.
- [**Speak**](scripted-speech/speak.md): record yourself reading sentences.
- [**Listen**](scripted-speech/listen.md): validate recordings.
- In Spontaneous Speech: [**Answer**](spontaneous-speech/answer.md) a question out loud, or [**Transcribe**](spontaneous-speech/transcribe.md) an answer.
- Moderators also use [**Question**](spontaneous-speech/question.md) and [**Validate**](spontaneous-speech/validate.md).
- The parts every step shares: [the contribution page](common-parts.md#contribution-page).

## Good to know {#good-to-know}

- The bar at the bottom of every page shows your points and your connection: [the status bar](common-parts.md#status-bar).
- Your numbers are on [the dashboard](profile/dashboard.md); your languages are on [your languages page](profile/languages.md).
- Need help? [Contact us](forms/contact.md).
- Your account menu is at the top right: [the account menu](common-parts.md#account-menu).
