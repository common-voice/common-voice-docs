---
title: How do I add a language?
description: How a language comes to Common Voice, and how to request a dataset for it
---

A language comes to Common Voice when its community starts translating the site on Pontoon; nobody needs to ask for it. To collect speech in your language, check the Dataset statistics page on [Common Voice](https://commonvoice.mozilla.org) for a dataset in it. If there is none, you can [request a dataset](dataset-request.md). There are two stages: translating the site, and collecting sentences.

## Need help deciding which to choose? {#which-dataset}

Common Voice has four sections, each with its own datasets:

- **Scripted Speech**: read sentences aloud in your language.
- **Spontaneous Speech**: answer questions in your own words, in a more organic, colloquial way.
- **Code Switching**: speak across two languages in one turn.
- **Asynchronous Conversations**: reply to others in moderated, topic-bound rooms.

You request a dataset for one section at a time. Requests are open for Scripted Speech; the other sections will open theirs later.

## Translating the site {#translating}

We use a Mozilla tool called [Pontoon](http://pontoon.mozilla.org/) for translations. Pontoon has lots of languages, but if it doesn’t have yours you can [request for your language to be added](https://pontoon.mozilla.org/teams/). Once Common Voice can be translated into your language on Pontoon, the language appears on Common Voice by itself within a few hours. [See more on site translation](localization.md).

[Watch our guide on how to use Pontoon.](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1YVyHUPaw2oiVdZZ7_pg607cIMq0YDMcw/view?usp=sharing)

## Collecting sentences {#collecting-sentences}

You can [add sentences on the Write page](../contribute/sentences.md#add-sentences), one or many at a time. For a larger collection, see [Larger collections](../contribute/sentences.md#bulk-submission). Remember that sentences need to be [CC0 (or public domain)](../contribute/guidelines/sentences.md#public-domain), or you can write your own.

A dataset needs a starting set of approved sentences before recording can begin. How many depends on how many people speak the language:

| Speakers of the language | Sentences to start recording |
| ------------------------ | ---------------------------- |
| Fewer than 1 million     | 750                          |
| 1 to 10 million          | 2,000                        |
| More than 10 million     | 5,000                        |

Recording also needs at least 60% of the site translated into the language. [See more on sentence collection](../contribute/sentences.md).

## In Progress {#in-progress}

These languages are currently under community development. [Website localization](localization.md) and [sentence collection](#collecting-sentences) is needed to launch. The Dataset statistics page lists them under In Progress.

## Launched {#launched}

For these launched languages the website has been successfully [localized](localization.md), and has enough [sentences collected](#collecting-sentences) to allow for ongoing Speak and Listen contributions. The Dataset statistics page lists them under Launched.
