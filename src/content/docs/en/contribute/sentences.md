## How do I add sentences? {#add-sentences}

In Scripted Speech, you can add sentences on the Write page or review sentences on the Review page.

Sentences must be reviewed and accepted by two people to be included in Common Voice. Sentences must be in the [public domain](guidelines/sentences.md#public-domain) and shorter than 15 words. You can ask the owner of a text to make it [CC0 using our waiver process](../datasets/data-rights.md#cc0-waiver), and send to us on <commonvoice@mozilla.com>

### What sentences can I add? {#what-sentences}

- No copyright restrictions ([cc-0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/))
- Fewer than 15 words per sentence
- Use correct grammar
- Use correct spelling and punctuation
- No numbers and special characters
- No foreign letters
- Include appropriate citation
- Ideally natural and conversational (it should be easy to read the sentence)

- [Sentence Collection](guidelines/sentences.md)

### How do I cite? {#how-to-cite}

**Cite with a URL link or the full name of the work.**

If it’s your own words, just say _“Self Citation”_. We need to know where you found this content so that we can check it is in the public domain and no copyright restrictions apply. For more information about citation see our [Guidelines page](guidelines/sentences.md#citing-sentences).

## Add a sentence {#single-sentence}

As a guest, the Write page offers **Add a sentence**: one sentence at a time, with its citation. The first time you contribute to a dataset, you agree once to release your contributions under the dataset's licence.

## Add sentences {#small-batch}

### How to add multiple sentences {#how-to-add-multiple}

Signed in, the Write page offers **Add sentences**.

- Follow guidelines from “What sentences can I add?”
- Add one sentence per line
- Separate sentences into one line by pressing “Enter” or “Return” once
- Add up to 50 sentences at a time to start; the number grows with your points on the dataset, up to 1,000
- All sentence must have the same domain
- All sentences must have the same citation

## Larger collections {#bulk-submission}

Have more sentences than you can add on the Write page, such as a public-domain book or a community corpus? Contact the team at [commonvoice@mozilla.com](mailto:commonvoice@mozilla.com). Your sentences should be copyright free (CC0 or permissioned original work by the submitter) and be clear, grammatically correct and easy to read. Submitted sentences should take roughly 10-15 seconds to read and should avoid including numbers, proper nouns and special characters.

## Review Sentences {#review-sentences}

Reviewing needs an account.

Make sure the sentence meets the following criteria:

- The sentence must be spelled correctly.
- The sentence must be grammatically correct.
- The sentence must be speakable.
- If the sentence meets the criteria, click **Yes** (or press Y).
- If the sentence does not meet the above criteria, click **No** (or press N). If you are unsure about the sentence, you may also **Skip** it (or press S) and move on to the next one.
- If the sentence is offensive or has another problem, use **Report** (or press R): see [Reporting Content](guidelines/reporting.md).
- If you run out of sentences to review, please help us collect more sentences!
