## Validate a question {#validate}

- ① Read the question.
- ② **Yes**: the question follows the guidelines. Key: **Y**.
- ③ **No**: it does not. Key: **N**.
- ④ **Skip**: you are not sure. Key: **S**.
- ⑤ **Edit**: change the wording of the question.

![The Validate page, with the question ①, Yes ②, No ③, Skip ④ and Edit ⑤](../../../images/en/manual/spontaneous-speech/validate-desktop-light.png)
