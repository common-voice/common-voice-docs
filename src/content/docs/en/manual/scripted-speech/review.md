Read a sentence and say if it follows the guidelines. The parts every contribution page shares are on [the contribution page](../common-parts.md#contribution-page).

## Review a sentence {#review}

- ① Read the sentence.
- ② The source is shown when there is one.
- ③ **Yes**: the sentence follows the guidelines. Key: **Y**.
- ④ **No**: it does not. Key: **N**.
- ⑤ **Skip**: you are not sure. Key: **S**.

![The Review page, with the sentence ①, the source ②, Yes ③, No ④ and Skip ⑤](../../../images/en/manual/scripted-speech/review-desktop-light.png)

## Tips {#tips}

- Open **Guidelines** to see the rules.
