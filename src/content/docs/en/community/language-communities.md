Many language communities run their own channels. Join one to meet other contributors in your language, ask
questions, and hear about events.

For the channels the whole project uses, see [Where to find us](index.md#channels).

## Add your community {#add-your-community}

To add your community's channels, or correct a link, open a pull request that edits this page. Keep the list in
alphabetical order by the language's English name, and give the language code in backticks after the name.
