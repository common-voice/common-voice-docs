There are three application forms. Each one asks a few questions in steps.

## Apply as an organization or project {#organization}

- ① Four steps: **About you**, **Your organization**, **Purpose of your application** and **Licenses and access**.
- ② Say who is applying: **Organization** or **Individual / Project**.
- ③ Type your full name.
- ④ Type the email where the team replies.
- Fields with a red star are required.

![The organization application, with the steps ①, who is applying ②, the full name ③ and the contact email ④](../../../images/en/manual/forms/apply-organization-desktop-light.png)

## The other two forms {#other-forms}

- **Apply to be a Community Facilitator**: use the link at the bottom of every page.
- **Apply for honorarium funding**: a form of the same kind.
