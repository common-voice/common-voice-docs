These parts are on every page. Other pages link here instead of explaining them again.

## The header {#header}

- ① The logo takes you to the home page.
- ② The main menu: **Contribute**, **Datasets** and **About**.
- ③ Your account: [the account menu](#account-menu).
- ④ The site language: [change the language](#site-language).

![The header, with the logo ①, the main menu ②, the account button ③ and the language button ④](../../images/en/manual/common-parts/header-desktop-light.png)

## The account menu {#account-menu}

- Open it with the picture at the top right.
- ① It shows who you are logged in as.
- Go to your [**Dashboard**](profile/dashboard.md) or your **Profile**.
- Press **Log Out** when you are done.

![The account menu, ①](../../images/en/manual/common-parts/account-menu-desktop-light.png)

## The site language {#site-language}

- Open it with the language button at the top right.
- ① Type to find your language.
- ② Pick your language from the list.
- It changes the website text only. Your contributions are not affected.

![The language list, with a search box ① and the languages ②](../../images/en/manual/common-parts/site-language-desktop-light.png)

## The contribution page {#contribution-page}

- Every step uses the same page: Speak, Listen, Write and Review in Scripted Speech; Answer, Transcribe and Review in Spontaneous Speech.
- ① Switch between the steps.
- ② Your dataset and how far you are.
- ③ The item you work on.
- ④ The buttons for this step.
- ⑤ Tools: **Guidelines** (the rules), **Shortcuts** (keys), **Tour** (a short walk through the page), the text style button (sans or serif) and **Waveform player** (the look of the player).
- ⑥ **Report**: tell us about a problem with this item. Key: **R**.
- ⑦ **Copy link**: copy the address of this page.
- ⑧ **Documentation**: open the manual for this page.

![A contribution page, with the steps ①, the progress ②, the item ③, the buttons ④, the tools ⑤, Report ⑥, Copy link ⑦ and Documentation ⑧](../../images/en/manual/common-parts/contribution-page-desktop-light.png)

## The status bar {#status-bar}

- It sits at the bottom of every page.
- ① Your points. Press them to see the points for each dataset.
- ② Your connection: **Online**, **Unreliable** or **Offline**.
- A storage gauge appears when your device is getting full.

![The status bar, with your points ① and your connection ②](../../images/en/manual/common-parts/status-bar-desktop-light.png)
