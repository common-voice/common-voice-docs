## What's Public? {#whats-public}

- We will not make your email public.
- Your name and points appear on a dataset's Top Contributors list only if you choose to be shown.
- Optionally submitted demographic data (e.g. age, gender, language, and accent) will never be made public on your profile, and will not be linked to your account in the dataset. Individual audio clips will be associated with demographic data for the purpose of more accurate analysis - for example, a researcher might want to target a training model to a specific demographic segment.
- Your username and email will not be associated with the published data.

## The CC0 license {#cc0}

It is very important that all text sentences are [public domain](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Public_domain) ([cc0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/)) as Common Voice language datasets are released under a cc0 license. Only ever upload a sentence if you are sure, and always include the relevant citation.

CC0 means the owner gives up every right to the work, so anyone can use it for any purpose without asking. Recordings and sentences in a language dataset are released under CC0. A domain-specific dataset can carry another license, so check the license shown for the dataset you download.

## Giving text to the dataset: the CC0 waiver {#cc0-waiver}

If you own a text, or you know who does, it can be dedicated to the public domain and added to the dataset.

1. The owner of the text (an author, a publisher, an organization) agrees to release it under CC0.
2. They sign the Common Voice contribution agreement for the text.
3. Send the signed agreement and the text to [commonvoice@mozilla.com](mailto:commonvoice@mozilla.com).
4. The team then adds the sentences to the dataset, as for a [larger collection](../contribute/sentences.md#bulk-submission).

## Your account and your recordings {#your-account}

You can [download your data](../manual/profile/download.md) and [delete your account](../manual/profile/delete.md) from your profile. Changing or clearing your demographic details is coming: see [Your account](../contribute/account.md#your-data). Releases that are already published stay as they were.
