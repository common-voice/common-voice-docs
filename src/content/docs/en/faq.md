## What is a language on Common Voice? {#what-is-a-language}

There are lots of ways to think about language. For the purposes of speech recognition models, Common Voice suggests focussing on ‘mutual intelligibility’, or ‘can speakers of this language mostly understand one another if they try to?’

- [What is a language on Common Voice?](languages/variants-and-accents.md)

## How do I add a language? {#add-a-language}

A language comes to Common Voice when its community starts translating the site on Pontoon; nobody needs to ask for it. To collect speech in your language, check the Dataset statistics page on [Common Voice](https://commonvoice.mozilla.org) for a dataset in it. If there is none, you can [request a dataset](languages/dataset-request.md). There are two stages: translating the site, and collecting sentences.

- [How do I add a language?](languages/index.md)

## How does site localization work? {#localization}

Translation of the Common Voice site happens on **Pontoon**.

- [How does site localization work?](languages/localization.md)

## How do I add sentences? {#add-sentences}

In Scripted Speech, you can add sentences on the Write page or review sentences on the Review page.

- [How do I add sentences?](contribute/sentences.md)

## How do I record a high quality voice clip? {#record-quality}

Speak in your normal voice! The way you speak is welcome here - we want your accent as it is, and we want your usual volume, style and intonation.

- [How do I record a high quality voice clip?](contribute/guidelines/voice.md#recording)

## How do I know whether to approve a voice clip? {#validate}

If you could **hear them** and **understand them**, it’s usually **best to approve**.

- [How do I know whether to approve a voice clip?](contribute/guidelines/voice.md#validating)

## How can we effectively grow a language on Common Voice? {#grow-a-language}

Creating opportunities for a diversity of people to contribute to Common Voice ensures the dataset serves as many people as possible. We’ve created resources and templates that you can use!

- [How can we effectively grow a language on Common Voice?](community/index.md#grow-a-language)

## How do I access and use the dataset? {#access-dataset}

Mozilla Common Voice datasets are now exclusively available on Mozilla Data Collective.

- [How do I access and use the dataset?](datasets/index.md#access)

## How are project decisions made? {#governance}

Mozilla Common Voice is made possible by a diverse community of activists, linguists, data scientists, academics and software engineers from all over the world. The project is stewarded by the Mozilla Foundation.

- [How are project decisions made?](about/index.md#governance)

## How is Common Voice funded? {#funding}

Common Voice is a project of the Mozilla Foundation, a US 501c3. The project is currently funded entirely by [philanthropic grants](https://foundation.mozilla.org/blog/mozilla-common-voice-dataset-grows-by-30-and-reaches-87-languages/), and donations from people around the world.

- [How is Common Voice funded?](about/index.md#funding)
