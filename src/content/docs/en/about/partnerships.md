It takes a lot to make Common Voice happen! We don't do it alone. Want to partner with us? We'd love to hear from you.

- [Get in touch](mailto:commonvoice@mozilla.com)

## Become a Common Voice Partner {#become-a-partner}

### Community, Creatives and Civil Society {#community}

The community is the beating heart of Common Voice - they come from all over the world, and are language activists and scientists and artists. You can learn more about how to get involved on our [About](index.md) page and in our [Community playbook](../community/index.md). If you're interested in deeper organisational partnership, [get in touch.](mailto:commonvoice@mozilla.com)

### Foundations {#foundations}

We work with foundations, civil society and community partners to make the internet a healthier place, with a particular focus on East Africa. Learn more about our [programmatic work.](https://foundation.mozilla.org/common-voice/in-country-programmes)

### Governments {#governments}

We are excited to support public sector engagement in technology, innovation and language rights. If you're planning to run a major project or campaign, please do [get in touch.](mailto:commonvoice@mozilla.com)

### Universities, Academics and Researchers {#academia}

Open innovation is at the heart of what we do. For research collaborations, data collection or interviews, [reach out.](mailto:commonvoice@mozilla.com)

### Small businesses, including tech and AI start ups {#small-business}

We are always looking to hear from data consumers about their needs and context - from collaborating on data collection platform to providing data health insights, we want to [hear from you.](mailto:commonvoice@mozilla.com)

### Large scale corporates and platform companies {#corporates}

If you're looking to speak more about the dataset, want to co-invest in planned platform expansions or look for collaboration opportunities, [drop us a line.](mailto:commonvoice@mozilla.com)

## Apply as an organization or project {#apply}

Signed-in users can apply on the Common Voice site to start working with us. The team reads every application and replies to the contact address you give. An application does not create anything on the site by itself: we set up your space together after we talk.

**Who is applying:**

| Choice               | Who it is for                                                                                                   |
| -------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Organization         | a body with a legal form: a university, a research institute, a non-profit, a company, a public body, and so on |
| Individual / Project | a person or a group without a legal entity: a researcher, a student working on a thesis, an independent group   |

An individual or a project can give an affiliation, such as a university, a lab or a supervisor. Your full name is asked so that the team knows who it is talking to; it is erased with your account. We also ask how to reach you and when: your title and how to address you, Matrix or Discord if you use them, the languages you prefer for correspondence, your timezone and a good time for a call. Of these, only your full name and email are required.

The licenses, visibility and contributors you expect are a first idea only. Each dataset picks its own when it is requested: see [Request a dataset](../languages/dataset-request.md).
