Add sentences that people can record. The parts every contribution page shares are on [the contribution page](../common-parts.md#contribution-page).

## Add sentences {#add}

- ① Type or paste your sentences, one on each line. As a guest you can add one sentence at a time; adding many at once needs an account.
- ② Choose up to three domains, if they fit. This is optional.
- ③ Say where the sentences come from.
- ④ Tick **Self Citation** if you wrote them yourself.
- ⑤ Press **Submit**.

![The Write page, with the sentences ①, the domain ②, the citation ③, Self Citation ④ and Submit ⑤](../../../images/en/manual/scripted-speech/write-desktop-light.png)

## Tips {#tips}

- Add only sentences that anyone may use: public domain.
- Keep them short and easy to read out loud.
- Rules for sentences: [Sentence Collection](../../contribute/guidelines/sentences.md).
