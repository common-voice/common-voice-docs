Anyone can preserve, revitalise and elevate their language by sharing, creating and curating text and speech datasets.

## Scripted Speech {#scripted-speech}

Read sentences aloud in your language and contribute to the most diverse public participation speech dataset in the world.

Recording voice clips is an integral part of building our open dataset; some would say it's the fun part too.

Validating donated clips is equally important to the Common Voice mission. Take a listen and help us create quality open source voice data.

## Sign up for an account {#account}

You can start without an account. As a guest, in a Scripted Speech dataset released under CC0, you can write one sentence at a time and record up to 100 sentences. Listen, Review, adding many sentences at once, and the other sections need an account.

### Benefits {#benefits}

- Make your submitted data as rich as possible by providing some anonymous demographic data. We de-identify all demographic data before making it public.
- Profile information improves the audio data used in training speech recognition accuracy.
- See your points on each dataset in the bar at the bottom of the screen.
- See how your progress compares to other contributors on each dataset's Top Contributors list.
- Your profile and dashboard: see [Your account](account.md).

### What's Public? {#whats-public}

- We will not make your email public.
- Your name and points appear on a dataset's Top Contributors list only if you choose to be shown.
- Optionally submitted demographic data (e.g. age, gender, language, and accent) will never be made public on your profile, and will not be linked to your account in the dataset. Individual audio clips will be associated with demographic data for the purpose of more accurate analysis - for example, a researcher might want to target a training model to a specific demographic segment.
- Your username and email will not be associated with the published data.

For technology to work well for everyone, the data that helps developers to build technology needs to reflect a diverse range of people. For example, if we don't include any speakers with a particular accent, then the technology won't work well for people with that accent.
