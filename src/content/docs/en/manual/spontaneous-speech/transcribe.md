Listen to an answer and write down what is said. The parts every contribution page shares are on [the contribution page](../common-parts.md#contribution-page).

## Write what you hear {#transcribe}

- ① The question the speaker answers.
- ② The sound of the recording.
- ③ Go back 5 seconds, play or pause, go forward 5 seconds.
- ④ Slower or faster playback.
- ⑤ **Auto** turns on automatic word-by-word playback.
- ⑥ Type what you hear.
- ⑦ **Skip** gives you another recording.
- ⑧ **Submit** sends your text.
- ⑨ The recording is kept for you until this time.

![The Transcribe page, with the question ①, the sound ②, the player ③, the speed ④, Auto ⑤, the text box ⑥, Skip ⑦, Submit ⑧ and the time ⑨](../../../images/en/manual/spontaneous-speech/transcribe-desktop-light.png)

## Tags {#tags}

- Type `[` in the text box to add a tag.
- Tags are **Disfluency**, **Laugh**, **Noise** and **Unclear**.

## Tips {#tips}

- Write the words as you hear them.
- Replay a part as often as you need.
- Rules for transcribing: [Spontaneous Speech](../../contribute/guidelines/spontaneous-speech.md#transcribe-the-audio).
