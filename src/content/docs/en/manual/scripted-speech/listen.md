Listen to a recording and say if it matches the sentence. The parts every contribution page shares are on [the contribution page](../common-parts.md#contribution-page).

## Check a recording {#check}

- ① Press play to hear the recording.
- ② This is the sentence they should read.
- ③ **Yes**: they read it correctly. Key: **Y**.
- ④ **No**: they did not. Key: **N**.
- ⑤ **Skip**: you are not sure. Key: **S**.
- **Yes** and **No** turn on after you listen to the start of the recording.

![The Listen page, with play ①, the sentence ②, Yes ③, No ④ and Skip ⑤](../../../images/en/manual/scripted-speech/listen-desktop-light.png)

## Tips {#tips}

- Answer **Yes** only if the words match what you hear.
- Something wrong with the recording? Use **Report**: [Report](../common-parts.md#contribution-page).
- Rules for validating: [Voice Collection](../../contribute/guidelines/voice.md#validating).
