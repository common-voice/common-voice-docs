See how much you have contributed, and when.

## Your numbers {#numbers}

- ① Pick **All types**, or one section.
- ② **Voice**: how many clips you **Recorded**, and how many were **Validated**, with the hours.
- ③ **Text**: how many sentences or questions you **Added**, and how many were **Validated**.
- Totals update within the hour.

![The Dashboard, with the types ①, the voice totals ② and the text totals ③](../../../images/en/manual/profile/dashboard-desktop-light.png)

## Over time {#over-time}

- ④ Pick an organization, or all of them.
- ⑤ Show the chart by day, month or year.
- ⑥ Show clips, hours or both.
- **Reset** puts your choices back to the start.
