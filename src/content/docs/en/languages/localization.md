---
title: How does site localization work?
description: Website Localization
---

The website text is translated into that language.

Translation of the Common Voice site happens on **Pontoon**.

[Create an account](https://pontoon.mozilla.org/accounts/fxa/login/) if you don’t have one. Then, choose your language **(‘Team’)** and then choose the project, [Common Voice](https://pontoon.mozilla.org/projects/common-voice/). There will be files to translate. Click on one, then it shows words in English and a box to translate them.

Translation is from English, but you can see **Suggestions** in other languages. Click the **Profile** icon, then **Settings** link and add any languages you speak. At the bottom right-hand corner will be a list of translations called **Locales**. Translations show on the site with its next update.

The site is ready to be launched in your language when 60% of it is translated and approved.
