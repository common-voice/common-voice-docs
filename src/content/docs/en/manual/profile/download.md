---

Your data is yours. Take a copy of it here.

## Your profile {#profile}

- ① **Download profile data** saves a text file at once.
- It has your email, your user name and your details.

## Your recordings {#recordings}

- ② Your recordings come as mp3 files, with a list that describes each one.
- ③ Pick a section, then press **Request recordings**.
- New recordings show here within the hour.
- When a copy is ready, it appears under **Your copies**. Press a part to save it.
