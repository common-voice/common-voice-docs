## General guidance {#reporting-content}

You must report content that is offensive, harmful or otherwise worrying. It must also include no personally identifying information like phone numbers or addresses.

In Scripted Speech, Report is on Speak, Listen and Review. Select the “Report” button (or press R) and choose what is wrong:

| Reporting   | Choices                                                                         |
| ----------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| A sentence  | Offensive language, Grammatical / spelling error, Difficult to pronounce, Other |
| A recording | Offensive speech, Difficult to hear, Personal information, Other                |

From here, provide more details about why you are reporting the content in the text area, and send it. The next item appears.

A reported sentence is no longer shown to you and waits for a moderator. A reported recording is no longer shown to you; votes still decide whether it goes into the dataset. A recording in the wrong language is not reported: vote No.

## Offensive Content {#offensive-content}

Sentences are vetted through a community-moderation process, however this process is not perfect. If you see or hear a sentence that offends or upsets you - for example because it violates our [community participation guidelines](https://www.mozilla.org/about/governance/policies/participation/) - please do use the Report button (or press R). You can also reach out to us at [commonvoice@mozilla.com](mailto:commonvoice@mozilla.com).
