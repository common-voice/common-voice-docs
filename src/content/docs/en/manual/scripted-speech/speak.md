Read a sentence out loud and send your recording. The parts every contribution page shares are on [the contribution page](../common-parts.md#contribution-page).

## Record a sentence {#record}

- ① The recorder shows your voice and the time.
- ② Read this sentence out loud.
- ③ Press **Start recording**, read, then press **Stop**.
- ④ **Skip** gives you another sentence. Key: **S**.
- ⑤ **Submit** turns on after you listen to your recording.

![The Speak page, with the recorder ①, the sentence ②, Start recording ③, Skip ④ and Submit ⑤](../../../images/en/manual/scripted-speech/speak-desktop-light.png)

## Check your recording {#check}

- ① Press play to hear your recording.
- ② Not happy? Press **Re-record**.
- ③ Happy? Press **Submit**.

![The Speak page after recording, with play ①, Re-record ② and Submit ③](../../../images/en/manual/scripted-speech/speak-recorded-desktop-light.png)

## Tips {#tips}

- Find a quiet place.
- Read the sentence as it is written.
- Rules for good recordings: [Voice Collection](../../contribute/guidelines/voice.md#recording).
