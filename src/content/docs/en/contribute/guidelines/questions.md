## Adding Questions {#collecting-questions}

Each dataset's moderators add and review its questions. This activity is opening soon.

### What makes a good question? {#good-question}

A good question for public participation datasets should:

- Be easy to understand and respond to
- Be generally relevant
- Not use, or solicit, harmful or offensive language

You might also want to consider spanning different contexts and domains.

### Easy to Understand {#easy-to-understand}

Choose simple questions that are easy for anyone to understand, regardless of culture or context.

### Spelling and Punctuation {#question-spelling}

Use correct spelling and grammar.

### Length {#question-length}

The question should be answerable in just a couple of sentences.

## Don’t Add {#dont-add}

### Culturally specific questions {#culturally-specific}

Questions which are very culturally specific, or make a lot of assumptions about the responder

### Length {#too-long}

Questions which someone would struggle to respond to in 15-30 seconds (the length of a good answer)

### Process / Steps {#process-steps}

Questions which require someone to list several steps. Listing steps prompts the user to recite an answer rather than a more natural response.

### Offensive content or sensitive information {#sensitive-information}

- Questions which might solicit personally identifiable information
- Questions which might solicit hate speech or other biased or offensive sentiments
- Questions which may cause someone to share sensitive, potentially triggering responses

## Example questions {#example-questions}

If you’d like to submit a tranche of questions for ingestion, email your list to [commonvoice@mozilla.com.](mailto:commonvoice@mozilla.com)
