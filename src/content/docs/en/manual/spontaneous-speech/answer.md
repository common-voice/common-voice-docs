Read a question and answer it out loud, in your own words. The parts every contribution page shares are on [the contribution page](../common-parts.md#contribution-page).

## Record your answer {#answer}

- ① The recorder shows your voice and the time.
- ② Read the question.
- ③ Press **Start recording**, answer, then press **Stop**.
- ④ **Skip** gives you another question. Key: **S**.
- ⑤ **Submit** turns on after you listen to your answer.

![The Answer page, with the recorder ①, the question ②, Start recording ③, Skip ④ and Submit ⑤](../../../images/en/manual/spontaneous-speech/answer-desktop-light.png)

## Tips {#tips}

- There is no right answer. Say it your way.
- After you record, the buttons work as on Speak: [check your recording](../scripted-speech/speak.md#check).
- Rules for answers: [Spontaneous Speech](../../contribute/guidelines/spontaneous-speech.md#answer-questions).
