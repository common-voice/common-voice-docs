title: Delete my profile
description: Delete your account and choose what happens to your recordings
---

You can delete your profile at any time.

## Delete {#delete}

- ① Choose what happens to your recordings: **Keep** or **Remove**.
- **Keep**: your recordings stay in the dataset, without your name.
- **Remove**: we also delete your recordings.
- After you delete your profile, you can no longer ask us to remove your recordings later.
- ② Type the number shown on the page.
- ③ Press **Delete Profile**.

![The Delete Profile page, with Keep or Remove ①, the confirmation code ② and Delete Profile ③](../../../images/en/manual/profile/delete-desktop-light.png)

## Changed your mind? {#cancel}

- Your account is deleted after a waiting time.
- Sign in during that time to cancel.
