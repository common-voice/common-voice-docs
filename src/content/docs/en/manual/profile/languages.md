Add the languages you speak. This helps others know how you can help. It does not change the site language.

## Describe a language {#describe}

- ① Press the language name to open its details.
- ② Choose the variant you speak, if there is one.
- ③ Describe your accent.
- ④ **Native speaker**: Yes or No.
- ⑤ **Expert**: Yes if you are a linguist, translator, editor or teacher.
- ⑥ **Level**: Beginner, Intermediate or Advanced.
- ⑦ **Remove** takes the language off your list.

![The Languages page, with the language ①, the variant ②, the accent ③, Native speaker ④, Expert ⑤, Level ⑥ and Remove ⑦](../../../images/en/manual/profile/languages-desktop-light.png)

## Add and save {#save}

- Under your languages, press **Add Language** to add another.
- Press **Save** when you are done.
- To change the language of the site, use [the site language](../common-parts.md#site-language).
